About Me

A look into who I am beyond the code editor.

I'm currently a 2nd-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science student at Lipa City Colleges, under the College of Computing and Technology Engineering (CCTE) department. My studies are grounded in programming fundamentals, data structures, and software engineering — the foundation I'm building on to become a well-rounded developer.

Right now I'm actively developing my skills in Full-Stack Web Development, with a growing interest in Machine Learning as a possible side path. Once I graduate, though, I plan to venture into Game Development — the field I've been most passionate about since I first started coding.

Leadership and communication have been a constant alongside my technical work. I've served as Class President from elementary through senior high school, held a position in our departmental organization, and currently write for LCC's The Vision Group of Publications as an Editorial, Development and Communication, and Literary Writer majoring in English.

Aside from my technical pursuits, you can usually find me playing video games, reading different literature, or even capturing amateur landscape photography, or exploring local coffee roasters when I have the time.
